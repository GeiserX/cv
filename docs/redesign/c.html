About

About

I started in telecoms, setting up routers for ISPs with TR-069 and zero-touch provisioning, and ran my own company doing GenieACS work for ISPs in four countries. From there I moved into DevOps and Kubernetes: 5G edge research, then four and a half years of Kubernetes managed services at Claranet, and now consulting at Xebia. Most of my own time goes into AI agent tooling, open-source desktop apps and a homelab that also hosts my blog. I live in Murcia and work in English and Spanish. Strong opinions, loosely held.
